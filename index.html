Welcome

Pembrokeshire Silage Day is a charity day out where you can watch real silage-making machinery at work – mowing, tedding, raking, harvesting and clamping – from modern harvesters to lovingly kept vintage tractors.

Raising money for charity name to be confirmed.

What to expect

🚗
ArrivingFollow the signs from the A487 by the Rising Sun Inn. Parking is in Car Park 1 or 2.
🎟️
The pay gatePay as you come in, by the car parks. Card and cash welcome.
🚜
Big, loud machinesHarvesters and mowers are very loud and move quickly. Everyone stays behind the rope lines.
🍔
Food & toiletsThe food village and toilets (including accessible) are in Field 5, the main show field.
🌿
Need a break?There's a quiet area away from the machines, and free ear defenders to borrow at the information point.
🌻
Here to helpOur staff recognise the Hidden Disabilities Sunflower lanyard – just ask if you need anything.
