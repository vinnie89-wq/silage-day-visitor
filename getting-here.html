Sat navs may take you to the wrong lane – follow the event signs once you're near.

By car

Head for the A487 just outside Haverfordwest. Turn into the lane by the Rising Sun Inn and follow the Silage Day signs.

Marshals will direct you to Car Park 1 or Car Park 2 (Car Park 2 is for two-wheel-drive cars). Parking is free / price to be confirmed.

From the car park, walk to the pay gate to come in.

Blue Badge holders

Accessible parking details to be confirmed. Please tell a marshal as you arrive and they will help.

The site is farmland – paths can be uneven, sloping and muddy after rain.
