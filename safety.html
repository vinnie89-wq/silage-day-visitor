Safety rules

Working farm machinery is big, fast and loud. Please read these before entering the demonstration fields.

  1. Stay behind the rope lines and barriers at every demonstration field.
  2. Never approach a working machine. Wait until it has stopped and the operator signals it's safe.
  3. Keep children with you and under close supervision at all times.
  4. Dogs must be on a short lead and kept away from demonstration fields.
  5. Watch for moving vehicles on the lanes between fields. Machinery has right of way.
  6. Follow instructions from marshals in hi-vis.
  7. No drones may be flown over the site.
  8. Wear sturdy footwear. Fields can be uneven and wet.
  9. In an emergency, tell any marshal or go to First Aid in Field 5.

Draft wording – Visitors attend at their own risk. Agricultural machinery is working on this site. The organisers will replace this with their own disclaimer.

🧒 Lost child?

Stay calm – lost children are usually found within minutes.

  1. Tell the nearest marshal or member of staff in hi-vis straight away. They'll radio event control.
  2. Go to the lost children point at First Aid in Field 5.
  3. Keep your phone with you and switched on.
  4. Found a child on their own? Stay with them and tell a marshal. Don't leave the site with them.
In an emergency call 999

Noise and comfort

Harvesters and mowers are very loud. Free ear defenders can be borrowed at the information point by the pay gate.

There's a quiet area by Car Park 1, away from the machines, if anyone needs a break.
